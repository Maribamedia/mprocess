The Model

Opportunity is everywhere. Verified, scalable revenue is not.

Most B2B growth stalls in the gap between a large market and a closed deal. Companies chase TAM they cannot reach. Funders back opportunities nobody has verified. Talent and skills sit idle next to mandates that cannot find them.

We do not sell leads or run outreach. We route: market, verified opportunity, revenue pipeline and compliant operations, in one motion.

Every route clears three tests before we introduce anyone. Is the opportunity real and sized? Is there a pipeline that turns it into revenue? Can the operator deliver, legally and operationally, at scale? Clear all three and we route. Miss one and we say so.

And we keep routing until fulfillment.

Common Questions

What decision makers ask first.

What is Mariba Media?

Mariba Media is a global routing layer. We connect B2B opportunities to revenue pipelines and compliant operations that can scale together, and we connect talent, funding, growth and skills to those opportunities.

What does "routing" mean in practice?

We read both sides of the market. When a verified opportunity is live, we route the funding, talent, suppliers or buyers that fit it. When capacity is live, we route the opportunity to it. Most operators pick a side. We do not.

How is this different from a broker, headhunter or lead-gen agency?

Those are paid for activity inside one lane. We are paid for the routing decision that converts. We research, verify, and make one filtered introduction at a time. No result, no success fee.

Which industries do you cover?

Energy and renewables, capital and financial services, infrastructure and construction, agriculture and AgTech, manufacturing and industrial, logistics and supply chain, healthcare and life sciences, technology and SaaS, and mining and natural resources.

Where are you based, and can you work across US, EU, Asia and Africa hours?

US mandates run on US hours from our Oklahoma base. Africa mandates run from our South African home base. EU and Asia mandates run through our cross-border desk, matched to the counterparty's working hours. Same routing layer everywhere; only the time zone changes.

What do you actually route?

Funding, talent and skills, and growth: suppliers, bids and buyers. Each is connected to a verified opportunity, a revenue pipeline and compliant operations. Purchase order funding is one instrument inside funding, not the whole model.

What does "until fulfillment" mean?

If the first curated introduction does not land, we route again under the same agreement, at no extra fee, until the requirement is fulfilled or the brief is withdrawn. Commercial outcomes remain with the counterparty.

What is the myoProcess model, and how does Mariba Media differ?

myoProcess showed that the leverage in B2B sits with whoever controls introductions, not whoever sends more outreach. Mariba Media applies that principle across funding, talent, skills, growth and supply, with a global theatre structure, two-way routing, a routing-until-fulfillment commitment and an explicit filter: value, demand, compliant capacity. The model is inspired by myoProcess; the rest is ours.

How do you get paid?

Three models: a fixed success fee upon fulfillment, a performance retainer credited against success fees, and a partnership negotiated per theatre. All fees are disclosed upfront and quoted in the currency of the theatre.

What does it cost to start?

Nothing. We research the market and tell you plainly whether there is an introduction worth making. If there is not, we say so.

Do you guarantee results?

We guarantee the routing process: signals exist in every market we operate, and curated introductions continue until fulfillment. We do not guarantee commercial outcomes. All terms, diligence and decisions remain with the counterparty.
