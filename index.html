Strategic Memo · On Routing, Not Outreach

Most routing runs one way. We run it both ways.

The market has supply. It has demand. What it lacks is a routing layer that reads both at once — and matches them through research, not volume.

Capital, talent and supply rarely find each other on their own. Supply cannot see demand. Demand cannot see compliant capacity. Most operators pick a side and route one way.

The leverage is not in outreach. It is in routing — supply to demand, and demand to compliant supply, in the same motion.

So we research first: who decides, what the live requirement is, and what compliance sits on both sides. Then we match. Only then do we introduce.

Three flows — funding; talent; supplies, bids and buyers — run on one routing layer, filtered the same way.

Value: does it work for both sides? Demand: is the requirement live, with a counterparty who can act? Capacity: can the supply side deliver, legally and operationally? Clear all three, we route. Miss one, we do not.

And we do not route once and leave. In every market we operate — US, EU, Asia — supply and demand signals are there. If the first curated introduction does not land, we route again. We keep routing until fulfillment.

We are not a broker. Not a headhunter. Not a lead-gen agency. We are a routing layer that runs both ways — and does not stop at the first introduction.

From the US, EU and Asia, we read demand in one theatre and route compliant capacity from another. US mandates run on US hours from Oklahoma. EU and Asia mandates run through our cross-border desk.

Because in some markets, one introduction is noise. In others, one introduction — routed the right way — changes a company.

Common Questions

What decision makers ask first.

Where are you based, and can you work across US, EU and Asia hours?▾

Mariba Process operates across three theatres. US mandates run on US hours from our Oklahoma base. EU and Asia mandates run through our cross-border desk, matched to the counterparty's working hours. Same routing layer in all three; only the time zone changes.

What does the guarantee cover, and what does "until fulfillment" mean?▾

The guarantee covers the routing process. In every market we operate — US, EU, Asia — supply and demand signals are guaranteed to exist and be readable; we do not open a market we cannot read. "Until fulfillment" means that if the first curated introduction does not land, we route again under the same agreement, at no extra fee, until the requirement is fulfilled or the brief is withdrawn. Commercial outcomes remain with the counterparty.

What does "two-way routing" mean in practice?▾

We read both sides of the market at once. When a demand signal is live, we route compliant capacity to it — funding, talent, supplies, bidders or buyers, whichever the requirement calls for. When compliant capacity is live, we route the demand signal to it. Most operators pick a side. We do not.

What do you actually route?▾

Four pillars: funding, talent (headhunting and pre-mandate search), supplies (compliant capacity routed to demand), and bids and buyers (tenders and enterprise procurement routed to the counterparty who can act). Purchase order funding is one instrument inside the funding pillar — not the whole model.

How is this different from a broker, a headhunter or a lead-gen agency?▾

Brokers, headhunters and lead-gen agencies are each paid for activity inside one lane. We are paid for the routing decision that converts — in whichever direction the demand sits. We do not sell databases. We do not run funnels. We do not run contingent search. We research the market, match requirements to compliant capacity, and route one filtered introduction at a time. If it does not produce a result, there is no success fee.

What is the myoProcess model, and how does Mariba Process differ?▾

myoProcess proved that the leverage in B2B sits with the person who controls introductions, not the person sending more outreach. Mariba Process applies that principle across funding, talent, supplies, bids and buyers — with a global theatre structure (US, EU, Asia), a two-way routing thesis, a routing-until-fulfillment commitment, and an explicit filter discipline: value, demand, compliant capacity. The model is inspired by myoProcess; the two-way routing, the guarantee and the vertical coverage are ours.

Which verticals do you cover?▾

Nine vital industries today: energy and renewables, capital and financial services, infrastructure and construction, agriculture and AgTech, manufacturing and industrial, logistics and supply chain, healthcare and life sciences, technology and SaaS, and mining and natural resources. We add new verticals as demand emerges — not as a marketing exercise. If your sector is not listed, tell us the requirement; we will tell you plainly whether there is a routing path.

How do you get paid?▾

Three models. Fixed success fee upon fulfillment — agreed in advance, triggered when the routed introduction reaches fulfillment, no result no fee, with routing continued at no extra fee until fulfillment. Performance retainer — an ongoing routing mandate, credited against success fees, available on demand. Partnership — based on demand, negotiated per theatre. All fees are disclosed upfront and quoted in the currency of the theatre (USD, EUR or local).

What does it cost to start a conversation?▾

Nothing. There is no cost and no obligation to open a brief. We research the market and tell you plainly whether there is a filtered introduction to make. If there isn't, we say so.

Do you guarantee results?▾

We guarantee the routing process: signals exist in every market we operate, and curated introductions continue until fulfillment. We do not guarantee the commercial outcome. All terms, diligence and decisions are the exclusive responsibility of the counterparty that engages with you. No funding, hiring, procurement or commercial outcome is guaranteed.
